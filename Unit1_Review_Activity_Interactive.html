Learning target

I am learning about the earliest forms of visual communication and how design history connects to careers today so that I can explain how the past still shapes the design work people do now.

  • 1I can identify early forms of visual communication and explain their basic characteristics.
  • 2I can sequence major developments in graphic design history in chronological order.
  • 3I can connect historical design developments to specific graphic design careers today.
  • 4I can justify how a specific historical era or invention influenced a graphic design career of my choice.
Working on this independently today
  • Go through the sections in order — each one gets a little more challenging, and later sections build on earlier ones.
  • Answer each question, then check it. You'll get feedback right away, so you always know how you're doing.
  • Keep your Unit 1 handout and timeline infographic nearby — you're welcome to use them.
  • Section 4 is a written reflection. Use the sentence starters — it gets included automatically when you submit your results at the end.
  • Be resourceful: if you get stuck, re-read the question, check your notes, and try again before raising your hand.
